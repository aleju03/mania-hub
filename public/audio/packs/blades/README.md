# Blade sounds

The supporter blades' cut and rip sounds (`src/components/packs/packBlades.ts` names them,
`packSfx.ts` plays them). All sources are CC0 1.0, so no attribution is required; the table
is provenance for whoever edits these next.

Katana and ember: Kenney's RPG, Sci-fi and Impact packs ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl)) and
OpenGameArt CC0 fire, trimmed and layered.

Lightning's cut, from Freesound:

| File | Layer | Source | Author | Freesound ID |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lightning-cut.mp3 | Zap | "Electric zap.wav" | michael_grinnell | [512471](https://freesound.org/people/michael_grinnell/sounds/512471/) |

lightning-rip.mp3 and all of frost, void, gale and ink use no recording: they are synthesized
from noise and simple waves: lightning-rip is a noise-only crack, an electric arc
sizzling down, a low thump and a short thunder roll; frost-rip ice cracking open and shards ticking
down; void-rip a reversed swell into a low boom; gale-rip a gust with leaves rustling; ink-rip a
wet splat and drops landing. Each cut is one short swish in the same voice; ink-loop is a
seamless brush-on-paper loop played while the ink blade moves, its level following the speed.

Processing: trimmed, faded, high-passed, mixed and limited; cuts peak at about -2 dBFS and
rips sit near -15 LUFS like the katana's; 48 kHz stereo MP3 (LAME VBR q2).
