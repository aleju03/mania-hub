# Third-party art in supporter frames

The shrine-and-magic frames (yinyang-orbs, reimu-ribbon, hakurei-torii, spell-card, witch-hat,
stardust, witch-broom, ice-fairy, scarlet-moon, rainbow-crystal-wings, time-stop-knives) use:

- LottieFiles animations under the Lottie Simple License (https://lottiefiles.com/page/license):
  "af magic circle" by Masahiro Kaneko, "3d rotating crystal" by Arslan Ali.

Everything else is drawn for this site: the green hat (witch-hat), the yin-yang orbs, the shrine ribbon, the shrine gate and
its blossoms, the broom and star bullets and the snowflakes. The
scarlet moon is a Blender model built and rendered for this site (its seas and craters come from
noise, not from a photo), and its bats are drawn here. The knives, clock bezel and second hand of
time-stop-knives are Blender models built and rendered for this site.

None of them uses art from the official Touhou Project games.

The sketchbook frames (pencil-boil, taped-page, margin-doodles, highlighter-loop, crayon-border)
use paper and tape scans from ambientCG (CC0 1.0, https://ambientcg.com): Paper001, Paper003 and
Tape005. The gacha frames' "SSR" letters and counter digits are set in Gabarito (SIL Open Font
License 1.1). The arcade, gacha and soul frames are otherwise drawn for this site, with no game
art, logos or sprites; the Susie's idea frame's pixel heart and lettering are drawn here too, but its
Susie head icon is the game's own battle-menu sprite (DELTARUNE, © Toby Fox; via the Deltarune
Wiki's Susie_face_battlemenu.png), used as fan art, not licensed.

The osu!mania frames added in October 2026 (note-highway, stair-roll, chordjack-pile, sv-gimmick,
percy-check, hit-error, hit-error-bad, hit-error-reverse, combo-break, results-grade, clutch-hp, skill-radar, seven-key) and dan frames
(black-belt, greek-circle, course-run, stage-four-fail, pass-stamp, trophy-shelf, dan-license) are
drawn for this site in code, with no game art or skin assets; the dan frames show the site's own dan
badges. Their lettering is set in Gabarito, Noto Serif Display and Noto Serif CJK JP (all SIL Open
Font License 1.1).

The CRT monitor frame's monitor is a Blender model built and rendered for this site, with no
external models, textures or images.
The cloud in the Rain cloud frame and the snow in the Fairy lights frame are Blender renders made
for this site.
The Crown frame's crown is a Blender model built and rendered for this site; the sky it is lit by is
Poly Haven's "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)" HDRI (CC0).
The Turntable frame's deck is a Blender model built and rendered for this site, with no controller
photos, game art or logos.

The Desktop icon, Paint canvas, Paperclip, Blue screen and Pipes frames (November 2026) are drawn for
this site in code, with no operating system artwork, icons, logos or screenshots.

The Flames, Viewfinder and Slim ring frames (November 2026) are drawn for this site in code, with no
camera maker's interface artwork, stock footage or textures.

The Blue flames, Lightning, Spectrum bars, Focus lines and Double line frames (November 2026) are
drawn for this site in code. The Sheet music frame is too; its clef is a glyph of Noto Music (SIL Open
Font License 1.1). The Jewel case, Fish tank, Stage curtains, Thorns and Chains frames are
Blender models built and rendered for this site, with no external models, textures or photographs.
