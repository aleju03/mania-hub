# Third-party art in supporter frames

The shrine-and-magic frames (yinyang-orbs, reimu-ribbon, hakurei-torii, spell-card, witch-hat,
stardust, ice-fairy, scarlet-moon, rainbow-crystal-wings, time-stop-knives) are composited from:

- Fluent Emoji by Microsoft, https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji, MIT licence
  (`fluentui-emoji.LICENSE.txt`): shrine, cherry blossom, broom, stars, moon, snowflake, red circle.
- LottieFiles animations under the Lottie Simple License (https://lottiefiles.com/page/license):
  "Spinning Yin-yang" by Krunal Padmashali, "af magic circle" by Masahiro Kaneko,
  "3d rotating crystal" by Arslan Ali, "Bat - Halloween" by Priyanshu Rijhwani.
- Openclipart (public domain): "Witch hat" by Lixaxy, https://openclipart.org/detail/218202/witch-hat.
- Kenney Particle Pack (CC0): star glints.

The knives in time-stop-knives and the shrine ribbon are drawn for this site.

None of them uses art from the official Touhou Project games.
