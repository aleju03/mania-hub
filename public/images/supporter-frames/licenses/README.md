# Third-party art in supporter frames

The shrine-and-magic frames (yinyang-orbs, reimu-ribbon, hakurei-torii, spell-card, witch-hat,
stardust, witch-broom, ice-fairy, scarlet-moon, rainbow-crystal-wings, time-stop-knives) use:

- LottieFiles animations under the Lottie Simple License (https://lottiefiles.com/page/license):
  "af magic circle" by Masahiro Kaneko, "3d rotating crystal" by Arslan Ali,
  "Bat - Halloween" by Priyanshu Rijhwani.
- Openclipart (public domain): "Witch hat" by Lixaxy, https://openclipart.org/detail/218202/witch-hat.

Everything else is drawn for this site: the yin-yang orbs, the shrine ribbon, the shrine gate and
its blossoms, the broom and star bullets, the snowflakes, the scarlet moon and the knives.

None of them uses art from the official Touhou Project games.

The sketchbook frames (pencil-boil, taped-page, margin-doodles, highlighter-loop, crayon-border)
use paper and tape scans from ambientCG (CC0 1.0, https://ambientcg.com): Paper001, Paper003 and
Tape005. The gacha frames' "SSR" letters and counter digits are set in Gabarito (SIL Open Font
License 1.1). The arcade, gacha and soul frames are otherwise drawn for this site, with no game
art, logos or sprites.
