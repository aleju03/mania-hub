The incumbent clears all four pass criteria: AUC >= 0.80, Holm p <= 0.05, survives
share adjustment while the exposure baseline collapses, and no control or placebo
comes within 0.05. LN Lean exactly ties it and therefore adds nothing over the
shipped axis. **Branch 4B: the 7K LN axis is valid as far as this test can see.
Ship nothing.** The r ~= 0.99 display correlation with Overall is an artifact of
where strong players sit, not invalidity.

Caveats: the protocol-correct high+medium primary collapses to 7v2 defined negatives,
because the three highest-Overall rice negatives (butanic, Kalkai, dressurf,
Overall ~35.5) sit in bands under the pre-registered 10-cell floor and get no D.
The 7K pass therefore rests on mid-band negatives (yz1155, tyrcs, plus the two
low-confidence names in the fuller run). n is small everywhere; this confirms a
strong effect, it does not estimate one.

Exposure alone separates the labels better than the incumbent. After share
adjustment nothing reaches 0.80 and the placebo partitions (release-load on
old maps: 0.833 raw) separate nearly as well as LN, the fingerprint of labels
tracking chart selection. The stricter analyzer-verdict partitions score *worse*
(0.54), so tightening the rating's chart filter is not a fix. **Branch 4A: ship
no new 4K axis.** The honest product option is surfacing LN play share next to
the 4K LN bar (or relabeling the bar "your level on LN charts"); a display-only
change, no recompute.

Release Load fails both strata on every variant. That design family is closed.

## What shipped anyway

- The top-LN-plays surface gate (`player-skills.ts`, `LN_PATTERN_LN_RATIO_MIN`):
  plays list under `pattern:ln` now requires the chart's stored classification
  `lnRatio >= 0.5`, the analyzer's own LN verdict. Display correctness, independent
  of the axis question. 76.3% of 4K charts tagged `ln` at 0.5 sit below that verdict
  (21,647 of 28,380, local snapshot), which is the gamma-chart complaint that
  started this.
- Prod data heal (Phase 0A) had already run: 176k frozen floor-value play ratings
  stripped, 4 residual poisoned plays at join time.

## Protocol deviations, logged

1. `score-labels.ts` does not implement the freeze file's confidence filtering;
   the high+medium primary and high-only sensitivity were run via filtered copies
   of the label file (`out/labels-primary.json`, `out/labels-high.json`).
2. The freeze file's referenced 2026-08-21 prod cells snapshot did not survive;
   the join ran on a fresh 2026-08-24 snapshot generated by the same script.
3. The remaining pre-registered sensitivity refolds (rate-1.0 only, top-only,
   truncation) were not run; the primary outcome was decisive in both strata and
   in the same direction as its own baseline comparisons.

## If anyone revisits this

More labels move the needle, nothing else does. 10+ per side per keymode enables
a holdout and a confirmatory tier; a second independent rater would address the
single-rater weakness the plan documents. The harness is rerunnable end to end:
`fold-cells.ts` (on prod, read-only) then `score-labels.ts --labels <file> --cells <prod cells>`.
